`abt.csv`, `buy.csv` and `matches.csv` are the Abt-Buy benchmark for entity resolution from the database group of
Prof. Erhard Rahm, University of Leipzig (https://dbs.uni-leipzig.de/research/projects/benchmark-datasets-for-entity-resolution),
made available "under the Creative Commons license" (the page names no version or variant), with the request to credit
the page and cite H. Köpcke, A. Thor, E. Rahm, "Evaluation of entity resolution approaches on real-world match
problems", PVLDB 3(1), 2010. `abt.csv` is `Abt.csv` converted from Latin-1 to UTF-8; `matches.csv` is
`abt_buy_perfectMapping.csv`. Otherwise unchanged.
