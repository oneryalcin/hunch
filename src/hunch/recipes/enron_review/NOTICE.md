(http://www.zlti.com) and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/). The source archive is distributed for the TREC 2010 Legal Track at https://trec-legal.umiacs.umd.edu/corpora/trec/legal10/ . `source_id` identifies the message in that archive.

The `gold` values are the final relevance judgments for TREC 2010 Legal Track topic 303, published by NIST at https://trec.nist.gov/data/legal10.html . The topic asks which messages relate to lobbying public officials about laws and regulations. We selected four yes and four no messages, removed routing headers and license footers, redacted contact details and a few names, and removed a repeated quoted header from `e07`. Otherwise the body text is retained, including its original spelling. None of these eight messages had a separately extracted attachment. The labels were assigned to the original full messages; redaction and selection can affect model results. This sample teaches the commands and cannot validate performance on the original collection. For the full corpus and its sampling-weighted evaluation, see https://fuguai.mintlify.site/cookbooks/document-review .
